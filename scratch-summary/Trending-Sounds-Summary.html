Trending Sounds: Project Summary

Automatic discovery, review and publishing of trending TikTok sounds

What this feature does

The system automatically finds sounds that are trending on TikTok, such as spoken memes, dialogues, sound effects and voice clips. It stores them in a private admin dashboard. Our team reviews them and publishes the good ones on the website, where visitors can listen and download the MP3.

What we collect for every sound

  • Sound name and creator name
  • Length of the sound (in seconds)
  • Views of the most-watched video that uses the sound
  • Countries where the sound is trending
  • How many different days the sound has been trending
  • First and last date it was seen
  • A day-by-day view history for the last 14 days
  • A ranking score. Sounds that keep trending day after day and in several countries score higher.
  • A link to the original video and the original sound on TikTok, so our team can verify every sound by hand

How often it updates

  • It checks 8 regions: Pakistan, India, USA, UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Indonesia and Brazil.
  • It updates automatically 4 times a day (about every 6 hours), with no manual work needed.
  • If a region does not respond, the system tries again after about 45 minutes.
  • The admin can also start a check at any time with one button.

Smart filtering

Not every sound is useful, so weak or risky ones are hidden automatically. Nothing is deleted by the filter. The admin can change these settings at any time.

Human approval

  • Nothing goes live by itself. Our team listens to each sound and decides to Approve or Reject.
  • Approving a sound saves its MP3 and creates its own public page.
  • Only approved sounds appear on the website.

What visitors see

  • A Trending Sounds section on the home page, and a Sounds page with search.
  • A page for each sound with a music-style player (play, pause, previous, next, progress bar) and a Download MP3 button.
  • A short description, FAQ and related sounds on each page.
  • Available in 11 languages.
  • A Report Content page for copyright or removal requests. Reports go straight to the admin inbox.

Important points to know

  • Views: the number shown is the views of the top video using the sound, not the total number of videos using it. That total is not available from our free data source.
  • Memes versus songs: the system decides mainly from the sound's name and numbers. It cannot hear audio, so our team makes the final decision by ear.
  • Copyright: a sound that feels like a meme may still have an owner, so we keep manual approval and a Report Content page.
  • Data source: we currently rely on one free public source. If it stops working, new sounds stop arriving, but everything already collected stays safe.
  • Search engines: while the site is on its testing domain, sound pages are not shown to Google. This will be switched on once the final domain is live.

Current status
