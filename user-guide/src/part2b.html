1.6 Listen before you choose

You cannot judge a sound by its name, so listen to it first. It takes a few seconds.

  1. Click Sound in the Check on TikTok column. The TikTok page of that sound opens. Press play and listen.
  2. Click Video if you also want to watch the most popular video that uses it.
  3. Come back to the admin panel and press Approve or Reject.

How to decide

Good to approve

  • Funny voices and short dialogues
  • Meme sounds that people repeat
  • Funny sound effects
  • AI-voice jokes
  • Clear, short sounds that people would like to download

Better to reject

  • Songs and music, even short parts
  • Advertisements and brand sounds
  • News and politics, or the voice of a famous person
  • Religious recitation
  • Clips cut from films, dramas or someone else’s work
  • Bad language, or just noise with no clear voice
Copyright A sound can belong to someone else, even when it is a famous meme. If you are not sure, press Reject. If an owner complains later, you can remove the sound at any time (section 1.8).
Tip You do not have to decide on every sound. Approve only the ones you like. The others can wait in the list.

1.7 Approve, Reject and Undo

ButtonWhat it does
ApprovePublishes the sound. The website saves the MP3 file, and after a few seconds the sound is on your website.
RejectHides the sound from your website and from your main list. You can find it later under Rejected.
UndoTakes your decision back. The sound goes back to “not decided”. If it was approved, it disappears from the website.

What happens when you press Approve

  1. The row changes at once: you now see Reject and Undo, and the words ⏳ Saving MP3…
  2. After a few seconds it changes by itself to ✅ MP3 saved, with the time it took and the file size. Now the sound is live. You do not need to refresh the page.
  1. 1MP3 saved. The sound is ready and live. (Here it took 1.1 seconds and the file is 145 KB.)
  2. 2Warning. The file could not be saved this time (for example “download failed”).
  3. 3Retry. Press it to try again. If it keeps failing, wait: the website also retries by itself after every automatic search.
  4. 4Undo. Takes the sound off the website.
  5. 5Reject. Takes the sound off the website and moves it to Rejected.

The Rejected list

  1. 1Undo. Moves the sound back to “not decided”.
  2. 2Approve. Changed your mind? Approve it straight from here.
Good to know Sounds you approved always stay in your Approved list, even if the filter would hide them. Sounds you rejected never come back by themselves. Changes appear on your website within a few minutes.

1.8 Reports from visitors

Every sound page has a link Report this sound, and the website footer has Report Content. When a visitor sends a report, it arrives in your admin panel.

  1. A small number 1 appears next to Messages & Inquiries in the left menu (Figure 7). Click it.
  2. Find the message whose label starts with Sound report (1 in Figure 8).
  3. Read what the visitor wrote (2 in Figure 8): the sound link, the reason and the details.
  1. 1The label. Shows it is a sound report and which sound.
  2. 2The report. The sound link, the reason (for example Copyright) and the visitor’s details.
  3. 3Buttons. Mark as read, archive, or delete the message.

What to do

  1. Open the sound link and listen to the sound.
  2. If the report is right: go to Trending Sounds, choose Approved in the list chooser, find the sound and press Reject. It leaves your website within a few minutes.
  3. Write back to the visitor by email. The website promises that a valid report is handled within 24 to 48 hours.
  4. Mark the message as read, or archive it.
Important Nothing is removed automatically. Only you can remove a sound. This also protects you from false reports.

1.9 Your daily routine (2 to 3 minutes)

  • Open Trending Sounds. Check that Last run is recent.
  • Look at the new sounds in Passed the filter. The best ones are at the top.
  • Click Sound to listen to the ones you like. Press Approve on the good ones.
  • Wait until each approved sound shows MP3 saved.
  • Open Messages & Inquiries and check for a new Sound report.
  • Once a week, open the Sounds page of your website and check that everything looks good.
