The admin panel

Where you choose the sounds

1.1 Open the Trending Sounds page

  1. Open your website address and add /admin at the end.
  2. Log in with your admin email and password.
  3. In the menu on the left, click Trending Sounds 1.
Tip You can also click Messages & Inquiries in the same menu to read reports from visitors (see section 1.8).

1.2 The top box

The first box on the page tells you if the automatic search is working.

  1. 1Turn OFF / Turn ON. Stops or starts the automatic search. Leave it ON.
  2. 2Run now. Searches for new sounds right now. It takes 1 to 2 minutes. You normally do not need it, because the website searches by itself 4 times a day (about every 6 hours).
  3. 3Status and Last run. “Status: ON” means it is working. “Last run” is the date and time of the last search. It should be recent (within about 6 hours).
  4. 4Counts. “99 sounds saved” is everything found so far. “29 pass the filter” is how many match your rules.
  5. 5Result for each country. The website checks 8 countries: PK (Pakistan), IN (India), US (USA), GB (United Kingdom), AE (UAE), SA (Saudi Arabia), ID (Indonesia), BR (Brazil). “ok 7” means 7 sounds were found there.
Good to know Sometimes one country says “failed”. This is normal. The next search tries again. If a red message says the last run failed in every country, do nothing: the website tries again by itself after about 45 minutes and keeps all your old sounds.

1.3 The filter

The filter is your assistant. It hides sounds that are probably not what you want, so your list stays short and good.

SettingWhat it doesNormal
1Min viewsA sound must have at least this many views on its most popular video.1,000,000
2Min secondsHides sounds shorter than this.3
3Max secondsHides sounds longer than this.60
4Per creatorShows at most this many sounds from the same creator, so one person does not fill your list.2
5Min days seenA sound must have been seen on at least this many different days. This keeps out sounds that were popular for only one day.2
6No songsWhen ticked, hides music tracks.ticked
7No brands/news/lyricsWhen ticked, hides company and brand sounds, news channels, song-lyrics pages, religious recitation, and famous people.ticked
8SavePress this after you change anything. Your list updates at once.
Safe to use The filter never deletes a sound. It only hides it from your main list. You can always see hidden sounds again (section 1.4).
Tip If your list is empty or very short, change Min days seen to 1 and press Save. You will then see every sound that matches your other rules, even those found only today. When you have more sounds, you can set it back to 2.

1.4 Choose which list to see

Above the table there is a menu with five choices. It decides which sounds the table shows.

ChoiceWhat you see
Passed the filterThe main list: sounds that match your rules and are waiting for your decision, plus the ones you approved. This is where you work.
ApprovedThe sounds you approved. These are on your website.
RejectedThe sounds you rejected.
Filtered outSounds hidden by the filter. Look here if a sound you expected is missing. The Filter column tells you why it was hidden.
AllEverything, in one list.

Each list shows the best 300 sounds, highest score first.
