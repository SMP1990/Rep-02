What visitors see

Where sounds appear on your website and how people download them

2.1 Where visitors find the sounds

Approved sounds appear in three places on your website.

The top menu

A Sounds link 1 in the top menu takes visitors to the list of all sounds. On a phone, it is in the menu opened with the three-line button.

The home page

The section Trending Meme Sounds is on the home page, just above “Free Online Tools”. It shows your 4 most viewed approved sounds. It appears only when at least one sound is approved.

  1. 1“Trending now” label and the heading of the section.
  2. 2A sound card. Click it to open the sound page.
  3. 3See all sounds. Opens the full list.

The Sounds page

This page lists every approved sound, with the most viewed first.

  1. 1Search box. Type a word to find a sound.
  2. 2Counter. How many sounds match.
  3. 3Play button. Plays the sound right here. Only one sound plays at a time.
  4. 4Sound name. Click it to open the sound’s own page.
  5. 5Download MP3. Saves the file to the visitor’s phone or computer.
Good to know A sound appears here only after you approved it and its MP3 shows “MP3 saved” in the admin panel.

2.2 The sound page

Every approved sound has its own page. It has a music player in the website’s colours. The speaker in the middle vibrates lightly while the sound plays.

  1. 1Previous. Opens the sound before this one in the list.
  2. 2Play / Pause. Starts or stops the sound.
  3. 3Next. Opens the next sound in the list.
  4. 4Progress bar. Shows how far the sound has played. Tap or drag it to jump forward or back.
  5. 5Time. On the left, how long it has played. On the right, the full length.
  6. 6Download MP3. Saves the sound as a file. It is free, with no sign-up.

Under the player, visitors also see the length of the sound and the number of views.

Under the player

The page also has a short text about the sound, an answer to common questions, and a list of other sounds.

  1. 1About this sound. A short text written automatically from the sound’s real details: its length, views, and since when it is trending.
  2. 2Open the video downloader. Takes visitors to your video downloader on the home page.
  3. 3Frequently asked questions. Four short answers, for example “How do I download it as an MP3?”.
  4. 4Report this sound. Opens the report form (section 2.4).
  5. 5More trending sounds. Links to other sounds.

2.3 How a visitor downloads an MP3

  1. Open your website and click Sounds in the menu (or click a sound on the home page).
  2. Press the round play button to listen.
  3. Press Download MP3, on the list or on the sound page.
  4. The file is saved. On a computer it goes to the Downloads folder. On a phone, look in Downloads or in the Files app.
  5. Open the file with any music or audio app. It also works as a ringtone or in a video editor.

2.4 Report Content

If someone thinks a sound should not be on your website (for example it uses their work), they can tell you. There are two ways to reach the form:

  • the Report this sound link on every sound page (the sound link is filled in for them), or
  • the Report Content link 1 at the bottom of every page of your website.
  1. 1Sound link or name. The visitor writes which sound (filled in automatically from a sound page).
  2. 2Reason. Copyright, harmful or offensive, their voice or private information, or other.
  3. 3Details. What is wrong, and who owns the sound.
  4. 4Name and email. So you can answer.
  5. 5Send report. The report arrives in your admin panel (section 1.8).
Good to know A visitor can only send a report. They can never remove a sound. A person (you) reads every report, and nothing is deleted automatically.

Questions and answers

The most common situations
QuestionAnswer
I approved a sound but I cannot see it on the website.Open the Approved list. Under the sound it must say MP3 saved. If it says “Saving MP3…”, wait a few seconds. If there is a warning, press Retry. After that, wait a few minutes: the website refreshes its list about every 5 minutes.
My list is empty or very short.Most likely Min days seen is 2 and the sounds are new. Change it to 1 and press Save, or wait a day or two. Also check that the list chooser says Passed the filter.
How often does the website look for new sounds?4 times a day, by itself. Press Run now to look right away.
“Last run” is old. What do I do?Press Run now. If the status says OFF, press Turn ON.
A famous sound is missing from my list.The filter may have hidden it. Choose Filtered out in the list chooser and read the Filter column to see why.
How do I remove a sound from the website?Press Reject (or Undo). It leaves the website within a few minutes.
Can visitors see sounds that I did not approve?No. Only approved sounds with a saved MP3 are shown.
A sound has huge views. Is it new?Not always. “Top video views” counts one very popular video, which may be old. Look at Days and Regions: many days and many countries means it is really trending now.
How many sounds should I approve?Start with 5 to 10 good ones. Then add a few every day. Quality is more important than quantity.
Will the sounds appear in Google search?Not yet. For now the sound pages are kept out of Google search, because your website is still on a test address. When the final website address is ready, they can be switched on. Ask your developer at that time.
Do I need to refresh the admin page?No. After you press Approve, the page updates by itself.
